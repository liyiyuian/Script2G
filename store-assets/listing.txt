SUBTITLE / TAGLINE
------------------
Run your own JavaScript scripts on your glasses


SHORT DESCRIPTION
-----------------
Add JavaScript scripts on your phone and run them on your G2 glasses: widgets as text, lists you
pick from, live updates. Many Scriptable scripts run as they are. An optional bridge on your own
computer fetches web data for them over your own Tailscale network.


DESCRIPTION
-----------
Run your own little programs on your glasses. Write or paste a JavaScript script on your phone, or import one from your computer, and run it from the list on your glasses. One example comes with the app: Time of day.

What your scripts can do:
• Show text, widgets, and lists you pick from with a tap.
• Update the glasses live while they run; long output scrolls smoothly.
• Keep values (Keychain) and files on your phone, use timers, and load your other scripts.
• Fetch web data through the optional bridge (below).
Scripts run in the app's own interpreter: instant for everyday scripts, slower for heavy number-crunching. Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Location, Photos and notifications aren't available.

Gestures: tap = run, swipe = move or scroll, double-tap = back (on the list: exit). Star a script to keep it at the top of the list and in the menu (tap then hold). A script can also open as soon as you launch the app.

Web requests: scripts reach the web through an optional bridge on your own computer, over your own Tailscale network, protected by a password. Install it with one command from github.com/liyiyuian/Script2G. Scripts that don't fetch anything need no computer.

Private by design: no accounts, no analytics, and the developer runs no server. Your scripts and Keychain values stay on your phone in the Even app (not the iOS Keychain, so treat them as less protected). A script you add can read those values and use your bridge, so only add scripts you trust.

Many scripts written for Scriptable work as they are. Inspired by Scriptable by Simon B. Støvring, made by a heavy user of it; not affiliated with it.

Requires the Even Realities app 2.2.10 or later. iPhone only for now.

PERMISSIONS (justifications)
----------------------------
network — Connects only to the bridge you install on your own computer, over your own Tailscale
network (https://<your-computer>.<your-tailnet>.ts.net:8447). The bridge fetches web data for your
scripts and shares a folder of scripts you can import. No other servers are contacted by the app.


PRIVACY POLICY
--------------
Full policy: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/liyiyuian/Script2G/main/store-assets/privacy.txt
In short: no accounts, no analytics, the developer operates no server and collects nothing. Scripts,
Keychain values and the bridge address/password are stored on your phone. Web requests go from your
phone to your own computer's bridge over your own Tailscale network, then to the sites your scripts ask
for.
