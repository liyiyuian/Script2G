evenScriptable (Script Runner) — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-10-14

Summary: the developer operates no server and collects no data. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

What the app stores, and where
- The scripts you add, the values you put in its Keychain section, files your scripts save with
  FileManager, and the address and password of your bridge are stored on your phone, inside the Even
  Realities app's storage for this app. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
- This storage is not the iOS Keychain and is not separately encrypted by the app. Scripts you add can
  read all of it, so only add scripts you trust.

Network
- The app's only network permission is to reach the bridge you install on your own computer, over your
  own Tailscale network. The developer never receives these requests.
- When a script makes a web request, your phone sends it to your bridge, and your computer fetches the
  address the script asked for. What those sites receive is up to the scripts you run.
- The bridge logs only the method, path and status of each request on your computer (no addresses,
  headers or contents).

Glasses
- The app shows text on your Even G2 glasses and receives your gestures. It does not use the
  microphone, camera, location or your Even account details.

Children
- The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Contact
